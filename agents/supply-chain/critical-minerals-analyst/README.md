# Critical Minerals Analyst Agent

> AI-powered supply chain intelligence for strategic minerals — powered by MineScope and CritMin Oracle

## Overview

This agent monitors and analyzes critical mineral supply chains using MineScope's intelligence platform and CritMin Oracle's on-chain verification. It produces risk assessments, geopolitical briefings, and supply chain concentration analyses for CFOs, procurement teams, and strategic planners.

## Use Cases

| Request | Output |
|---------|--------|
| "Lithium supply risk from Chile" | Geopolitical + on-chain risk score with recommendations |
| "Top 5 concentrated supply chains" | HHI analysis by mineral and country |
| "Track new IRA critical mineral rules" | Regulatory impact assessment |
| "Rare earth price alert China" | Event-driven risk briefing |
| "Compare battery supply paths" | Scenario analysis: Chile vs Australia vs Argentina |
